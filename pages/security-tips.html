Cybersecurity Tips

Simple security practices to protect your accounts, devices and personal information.

🔐 Password Security

✅ Use long and unique passwords.
✅ Use a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special characters.
✅ Never reuse the same password for important accounts.
✅ Use a password manager to securely store passwords.
❌ Never share your password with other people.

👤 Account Security

✅ Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
✅ Review your account login activity regularly.
✅ Keep your recovery email and phone number updated.
❌ Never share verification codes with anyone.

📱 Device Security

✅ Keep your operating system and applications updated.
✅ Use screen locks such as PINs, passwords or biometrics.
✅ Install applications only from trusted sources.
❌ Avoid installing unknown or suspicious software.

🌐 Safe Browsing

✅ Check website addresses before entering sensitive information.
✅ Look for HTTPS when visiting websites.
✅ Be careful with links received through email and messages.
❌ Do not download files from unknown websites.

🎣 Avoid Phishing

⚠️ Be suspicious of unexpected emails asking for personal information.
⚠️ Check the sender's email address carefully.
⚠️ Do not click suspicious links.
⚠️ Never provide passwords or OTPs through suspicious messages.
⚠️ Remember: Cybersecurity is not only about technology. Your everyday decisions can help protect your personal information and online accounts.
