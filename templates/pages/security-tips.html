STAY SAFE ONLINE

Cybersecurity Awareness

Learn simple but effective ways to protect your accounts, devices and personal information.

🔐

Password Security

Protect your passwords and credentials.

  • Use long and unique passwords.
  • Combine uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special characters.
  • Never reuse the same password for important accounts.
  • Use a password manager to securely store passwords.
  • Never share your password with other people.
👤

Account Security

Keep your online accounts protected.

  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
  • Review your account login activity regularly.
  • Keep your recovery email and phone number updated.
  • Never share verification codes with anyone.
📱

Device Security

Keep your devices safe from threats.

  • Keep your operating system and applications updated.
  • Use screen locks such as PINs, passwords or biometrics.
  • Install applications only from trusted sources.
  • Avoid installing unknown or suspicious software.
🌐

Safe Browsing

Browse the internet carefully.

  • Check website addresses before entering sensitive information.
  • Look for HTTPS when visiting websites.
  • Be careful with links received through emails and messages.
  • Do not download files from unknown websites.
🎣

Avoid Phishing

Recognize suspicious messages and links.

  • Be suspicious of unexpected emails asking for personal information.
  • Check the sender's email address carefully.
  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Never provide passwords or OTPs through suspicious messages.
⚠️

Remember: Stay Alert, Stay Protected

Cybersecurity is not only about technology. Your everyday decisions play an important role in protecting your personal information and online accounts.
